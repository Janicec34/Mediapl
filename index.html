Creative Statement

I am an artist interested in digital art, particularly in the world of character designing. I enjoy creating original characters because I find the process to be both joyful and satisfying. My work often includes researching different cultures around the world and taking inspiration from them to create unique characters. I mainly use drawing apps such as Procreate to bring these ideas to life. I am inspired by several artists I discover through different social media platforms, as well as my friends. I enjoy experimenting with different art styles and color palettes to find new ways to express my ideas.

I want my work to make the viewer curious about the characters I create. I also hope my characters can introduce viewers to different cultures and encourage them to learn more about them. I enjoy researching different cultures and incorporating elements of them into my character designs while also trying to be respectful and thoughtful about their meaning. I want each character to give a sense of “wow” to not only the viewer, but also myself. Through this course, I hope to improve my overall drawing skills. I also want to open myself up to new mediums and learn how to work with them. I think experimenting with different forms of art can help me discover new ways to express my ideas and develop my characters. Ultimately, I want to continue growing as an artist while creating work that is enjoyable for me to make and interesting for others to explore.

Artist Statement

I really enjoyed the type of work that was shown by Sara Stern. I was able to tell that she enjoys using the environment as an addition to her artwork. The first artwork that was shown was very interesting as I honestly didn’t know about the history of the Columbiana University’s new building. Her work of being able to turn architecture into a form of art by combining it with puppets really impressed me, I never thought about art in that form before. It links back into the trend about transplants and gentrification, and how people dislike having new people moving into the city or creation of new modern buildings in places that they either went to or grew up with. Having the building be able to speak also gave it more sympathy, like having a soul.

The second one I want to talk about is Mirrorball. Not only that I thought that the movement and the puppet design were very creative, I also thought that it was a bit silly; mainly due to the movement of the puppets. Stern continues to use the environment—in this case, the dunes—to build upon the art. The use of the instrument was also important as it gave it an idea similar to old school video games and their “desert-ish” original sound track. I also think that Mirrorball is a great representation of queer history. The two dolls with identical clothing and halve disco balls for head could represent how generally gay men (assuming that they are masculine) share the exact same mannerisms, fashion sense, or interests.
